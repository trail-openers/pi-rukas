feat(lean): read the contract through the accessor in every step

specify, build, challenge, verify, publish and the four review renderers
now read their spec through specFor, so a run whose contract the engine
derived is graded against the contract it is actually executing instead of
being declined for an issue that never carried a block.

publish-body prints a `spec: derived` line when the record says so, and
ready posts the derived contract to the issue the moment it mints it —
skipped on the host channel, which owns its own delivery.
